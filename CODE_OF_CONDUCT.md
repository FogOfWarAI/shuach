# Code of conduct

People of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome to contribute code,
documentation, data-adapter designs and replication results. In reviews and
discussions, describe the behavior you observed, share enough context for
someone else to check it, and direct criticism at the work. Credit others for
their contributions and respect requests to end an interaction.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks and disclosure
of another person's private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

Use GitHub's moderation and reporting controls on the relevant conversation.
If you need help from a maintainer, request a private follow-up and share
personal details only in that private conversation.
